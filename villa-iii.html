The villa

Villa III, in detail

Pebbles III, the newest villa of the collection, opened in 2021 and was designed with modern architecture in mind while keeping traditional Mykonian lines. Six double bedrooms, each with its own bathroom, combine a grand style with the Pebbles standard of elegance and minimalism. Like its neighbours it has daily maid service, green grounds rare for the island and an ecological pool without chemicals, finished with a cascade. The terrace looks over the south coast towards Paros and Naxos — a villa for up to 13 guests who want space and privacy.
